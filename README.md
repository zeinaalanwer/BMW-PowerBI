# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze car sales performance across multiple years, countries, regions, and sales channels.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor revenue, sales quantity, top-performing car models, and business trends through dynamic visualizations and filters.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales performance over time.
- Track total revenue and quantity sold.
- Compare current year performance with previous years.
- Identify top-selling car models.
- Explore sales by country, region, and channel.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Revenue Analysis
- Total Revenue by Year
- Revenue Previous Year Comparison (PV_Y)
- Revenue Trend Analysis

### Sales Quantity Analysis
- Total Quantity Sold
- Quantity Previous Year Comparison
- Year-over-Year Performance Tracking

### Product Performance
- Top Selling Car Models
- Product Ranking

### Geographic Analysis
- Sales by Country
- Regional Performance Analysis

### Interactive Filters
Users can filter data by:
- Year
- Car Model
- Country
- Region
- Sales Channel

## 📌 Key Insights
- Identify best-performing car models.
- Monitor revenue growth trends.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Evaluate channel effectiveness.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
